## Design conflicts requiring careful interpretation

- Root/A/B baseline uses `#ED101A`, SF Pro/system typography, 56px display headings, 80px section spacing and pill CTAs. It also explicitly says the approved selected page takes precedence over the baseline.
- `docs/design.md` begins with the spelling `IKONECTTI` and names `Ikonectt Telecommunications Limited`, conflicting with the editorial draft's Ikonectt brand/Intelivision Technologies company relationship. Do not carry either discrepancy into new copy.
- The expanded document includes 99.99% uptime in a status-token comment, four symmetric commercial tiers, zero FUP limits, network/peering/licensing assertions, a supposedly verified Victoria Island address and production portal telemetry/payment/reboot examples. These are design examples, not evidence of implemented services or current facts.
- Its SF Pro/Plus Jakarta restriction conflicts with existing D's Manrope and E's Outfit and their existing red treatments. The current user explicitly requires five distinct visual identities, so preserve D/E typography and composition.
- Existing blue/green status indicators do not justify decorative new colours. Status must remain understandable without colour alone.
- No new homepage composition or generated imagery belongs in this phase.

Do not reuse the unlimited-data answer until current FUP terms are verified. The source answer saying current prices are listed in a catalogue needs a preview qualification because no verified catalogue is supplied. Likewise, the source renewal/account answer must disclose that My Ikonectt is a preview and cannot perform account actions here.

The user directly supplies the Help Centre heading **How can we help?** and supporting line **Find answers, troubleshoot a problem or contact our support team.** Reuse those without expansion. Source validation wording includes `Please complete this field.`, `Enter a valid email address.`, `Enter a valid phone number.` and `Select an option to continue.`

Source success messages such as “Your enquiry has been received” describe future connected functionality. For Phase 1, use a concrete qualifier such as **Demo complete. Your details have not been sent.** Do not store form data/passwords in browser storage or make external submissions merely to simulate success.
