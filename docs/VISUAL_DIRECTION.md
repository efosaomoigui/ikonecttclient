# Abstract connectivity imagery

Client reference, 20 October 2026: real aerial city, road and interchange photography with luminous network links and nodes integrated into the physical scene.

For future abstract imagery, favour a believable photographic environment with restrained connectivity overlays, visible depth and coherent routes. Keep the subject recognisable and the composition readable. Match the active template's palette; the reference's blue light is an example, not a requirement to replace Ikonectt's burgundy/red treatment.

Avoid relying solely on generic wire patterns, floating connections without context, or uniform synthetic city blocks. The reference is visual direction for future work, not an instruction to redesign the current approved hero during the presentation push.
