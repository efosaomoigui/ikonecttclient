Frequently Asked Questions

Is iKonectt Residential Internet truly unlimited with no data caps? expand_more
Yes, absolutely. All iKonectt residential packages operate on a true unmetered policy. There are zero fair-usage throttle caps, no peak-hour speed rollbacks, and no rollover expirations. You receive full advertised speeds 24/7.
How fast is the installation process once I order? expand_more
If your estate or residential street already has active iKonectt optical distribution boxes, our field team completes line splicing and Wi-Fi configuration within 24 to 48 hours of subscription confirmation.
What hardware is provided during initial installation? expand_more
Every setup includes a carrier-grade Optical Network Terminal (ONT) and a dual-band Wi-Fi 6 wireless router capable of handling multi-gigabit wireless transmission with low attenuation.
How does weather or heavy rain affect fiber connectivity? expand_more
Unlike microwave or wireless 4G/5G links, optical fiber cables transmit data via pulses of pure light through armored underground glass conduits. Heavy downpours, wind, and thunderstorms have zero impact on your speed and latency.
