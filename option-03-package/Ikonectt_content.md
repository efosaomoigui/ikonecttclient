## 0. Reading rules and approval statuses

- **[APPROVED-DIRECTION]** Previously established design/architecture direction; not a claim that the client formally signed off.
- **[PROPOSED-COPY]** Concise editorial wording authored for the redesign; may be used in a prototype, subject to client editorial approval.
- **[EXISTING-SOURCE]** Existing company content found on its public websites, quoted minimally or paraphrased and linked to its source.
- **[VERIFY]** Do not publish as fact until confirmed by Ikonectt.
- **[DYNAMIC]** Must come from live data, verified catalogue, authorised account, or a controlled mock clearly marked DEMO.
- **[LEGAL]** Do not shorten, invent or overwrite contractual or statutory text without client/legal approval.

**Core rule:** Never confuse attractive copy with verified fact. Use placeholders only for genuine missing data, never for invented service benefits. The words `fast`, `reliable`, `fibre`, `home`, `business` may be used in ordinary brand language, but no absolute performance guarantees or specific numerical claims without verification.

## 2. Editorial strategy and message ownership

**Tone:** modern, warm, concise, credible, confident, restrained. One message per section. Short lines. Strong photography. No excessive claims, repeated paragraphs or generic AI superlatives.

| Message | Primary owning page | Other pages may do |
|---|---|---|
| Overall brand proposition | Homepage | Briefly reference and link |
| Everyday home use | Residential | One-line teaser on homepage |
| Business requirements | Business | One-line teaser on homepage |
| Plans, speeds, prices, allowances | Plan catalogue/detail | Show data-driven preview cards |
| Service availability | Coverage | Link to single coverage workflow |
| Installation process | Residential enquiry / Support | Short teaser only |
| Company background and partnerships | About | One brief confidence statement if verified |
| Help, FAQs and troubleshooting | Support | Relevant contextual link |
| Account services and payments | My Ikonectt | Login CTA only |

**Copy length guide:** Hero heading 3–8 words; hero description 12–22 words; section heading 3–8 words; section description 10–25 words; card body 8–20 words; FAQ answer 20–65 words where feasible. Longer material belongs in product detail, legal, or knowledge articles.

**No duplication rule:** A full coverage checker appears only in the dedicated coverage journey (the homepage may have a compact launcher). A full enquiry form appears on enquiry/contact pages, not in every section. Plan facts come from one catalogue. Do not repeat identical benefit cards across the homepage, Residential and Business pages.
