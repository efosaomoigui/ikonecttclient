# IKONECTT — DESIGN OPTION 03
## Complete Antigravity Design Brief and Homepage Blueprint

**Purpose:** Create a third, independently reviewable Ikonectt homepage inspired by the *visual restraint* of the supplied Starlink screenshot. This document combines the design direction, all homepage sections, copy, visual specifications, content controls, and implementation instructions in one place.

**Priority:** This is a design exploration for client comparison. Do not overwrite Design Options 01 or 02. Use `Ikonectt_design.md`, `Ikonectt_content.md`, the official logo assets and the supplied screenshot in the accompanying ZIP as references. Where the current approved content differs from draft text below, `Ikonectt_content.md` takes priority. Never invent prices, speeds, statistics, addresses, testimonials, trials, or service promises.

## 1. Visual direction

- Reference: Starlink screenshot's bold, concise headings; near-black canvas; restrained plan cards; substantial whitespace; editorial imagery; alternating storytelling sections.
- Interpretation: an original premium Nigerian fibre-broadband experience in Ikonectt's identity, **not a Starlink clone**. Avoid satellite hardware imagery and satellite-service language.
- Palette: charcoal `#101010` / near-black `#090909`, white `#FFFFFF`, pearl `#F5F5F7`, official Ikonectt red (the design file currently proposes `#ED101A`, verify against the official logo).
- Typography: SF Pro-style system typography, strong headline scale, short supporting copy, minimal microcopy. No excessive decorative icons or shadows.
- Grid: desktop design at 1440px; centered content max-width around 1280px; consistent 64–80px desktop gutters, 32px tablet, 20px mobile. Major sections approximately 80–104px vertical rhythm; adapt responsively.
- Imagery: high-quality, natural Nigerian homes and professional business settings. Select images for the actual layout and crop. No low-resolution AI faces, distorted hands, or repeated stock photos.
- Motion: subtle fade/reveal and hover, no excessive animation; respect reduced motion.

## 2. Non-negotiable design rules

1. **No form, postcode field, coverage selector, plan selector or enquiry input in the hero.** The hero contains an eyebrow, headline, one supporting sentence and two CTAs only.
2. Hero is **one continuous full-bleed photographic composition**, not a separate right-hand portrait card.
3. Maintain the exact brand spelling **Ikonectt**, the original logo without distortion/italics, and portal name **My Ikonectt**.
4. Residential and Business receive equal prominence.
5. Desktop Residential and Business navigation use **real, accessible multi-column mega menus**, not small generic dropdowns.
6. No unsupported claims about coverage, installation times, uptime, pricing, customer numbers, network partners or free trials.
7. Do not add testimonials, newsletter signup or a 30-day trial without verified company content and approval.
8. Preserve the approved architecture and navigation; this phase is the public homepage, not the Admin or authenticated portal.

## 3. Complete homepage — section-by-section

### 00 — Global navigation
**Look:** Dark utility bar for audience switching above a clean main header. Official logo on the left; Residential, Business, Coverage, Support, About Us; clear red-accented **My Ikonectt** action on the right.

**Residential mega menu:** Overview, Plans, Compare Plans, Check Coverage, Installation Enquiry; one restrained CTA panel.

**Business mega menu:** Overview, Business Solutions, Business Plans, Request a Quote, Check Coverage; one restrained CTA panel. Do not present speculative products as existing services.

**Interaction:** hover and click, keyboard navigation, focus management, Escape and outside-click dismissal, mobile adaptation.

### 01 — Cinematic full-width hero
**Visual:** Edge-to-edge premium Nigerian connected-lifestyle photo, dark cinematic grading and natural negative space for readable overlay text. Avoid a split layout. Image and text should feel integrated.

**Eyebrow:** FIBRE BROADBAND

**Headline:** Connectivity without limits.

**Supporting line:** Reliable fibre broadband for homes, businesses and everything you do online.

**Primary CTA:** Explore Residential

**Secondary CTA:** Explore Business

**Strictly no form or input in this section.** A coverage link can live in the header and the dedicated coverage section below.

### 02 — Choose your connection
**Look:** Spacious dark section with two equally weighted large photographic panels; Home and Business. Use original image compositions with matched card heights and aligned text.

**Section heading:** Internet for every possibility.

**Residential title:** At Home

**Residential line:** Internet for the way you live.

**CTA:** Explore Residential

**Business title:** At Work

**Business line:** Connectivity for business.

**CTA:** Explore Business

### 03 — Plans
**Look:** Dark charcoal section; large heading; restrained cards with clear hierarchy. If verified catalogue data is unavailable, show audience-level pathways rather than fictional priced plans.

**Heading:** Find your connection.

**Supporting line:** Explore available plans for your home or business.

**Cards:** Residential Plans / Business Plans, each with a brief description and an action. Once verified, use actual plan names, speeds, prices, billing periods and relevant attributes from the approved catalogue.

**CTA:** View All Plans

### 04 — Residential lifestyle editorial
**Look:** One deliberate light pearl section that breaks the dark rhythm; large immersive home/work-from-home image; left/right editorial composition, not an icon grid.

**Heading:** Made for the moments that matter.

**Supporting line:** From workdays to downtime, discover connectivity that fits into everyday life.

**CTA:** Explore Residential

### 05 — Business connectivity editorial
**Look:** Return to charcoal; premium real Nigerian office/workplace image, generous margins and crisp typography. Vary composition from the Residential section to avoid repetitive alternation.

**Heading:** Keep your business connected.

**Supporting line:** Explore broadband solutions for your business needs.

**CTA:** Explore Business

### 06 — Coverage action
**Look:** Focused dark/charcoal or restrained red-accent section. One strong headline and CTA. **Do not repeat the full coverage form on the homepage.**

**Heading:** Is Ikonectt available at your address?

**Supporting line:** Check service availability in your area.

**CTA:** Check Coverage

**Destination:** `/coverage`, which contains the actual location hierarchy and availability checker.

### 07 — My Ikonectt self-service
**Look:** Dark premium product-editorial section; if authentic portal screenshots are unavailable, use an elegant neutral composition, not a fake production dashboard screenshot.

**Heading:** Your connection. Your control.

**Supporting line:** Manage your services, payments and support through My Ikonectt.

**CTA:** Go to My Ikonectt

**Destination:** `/my-ikonectt/login`. No public registration CTA.

### 08 — Support
**Look:** Minimal light section with a strong typographic message and one direct action; avoid generic icon grids.

**Heading:** Here when you need us.

**Supporting line:** Find answers, get help and reach the right team.

**CTA:** Visit Support

### 09 — Comprehensive footer
**Look:** More complete and structured than the supplied Starlink footer. Dark charcoal, clear column hierarchy, whitespace and a restrained separator.

**Elements:** Official logo; one short brand line; grouped links for Residential, Business, Coverage, Support, Company, My Ikonectt; verified address, email and telephone only; legal links; verified social links; copyright.

**Short brand line:** Connecting homes, businesses and possibilities.

**Do not** invent contact details or fill the footer with long company descriptions.

## 4. Photography and asset checklist

- **Hero:** panoramic 16:9 or wider, contemporary Nigerian lifestyle setting, subject naturally embedded on right or center-right, dark readable negative space left, cinematic but believable.
- **Residential panel:** premium Nigerian family/home, warm realistic interior, portrait/vertical crop.
- **Business panel:** contemporary Nigerian team/workspace, editorial portrait/vertical crop.
- **Residential editorial:** home-office or connected-lifestyle scene, wide crop, bright natural light.
- **Business editorial:** modern Nigerian workplace or professional collaboration, wide crop.
- **My Ikonectt:** authentic UI screenshot only if available; otherwise request a dedicated art-direction brief or use a restrained abstract surface without fake data.

Before replacing assets, inventory what already exists. Reuse suitable approved assets; list missing images with exact intended section, crop, subject, negative space and lighting. Ask for new images rather than silently choosing unsuitable placeholders.

## 5. Content and architecture rules

- `Ikonectt_content.md` is the primary editorial reference. The lines in this brief are the **Option 03 proposed homepage copy**, subject to the content file and approval.
- `Ikonectt_design.md` is the design-token baseline. The Option 03 visual direction may adapt the layout but must retain brand identity.
- Do not generate filler sections, invented claims or repeated slogans.
- Public website remains separate from My Ikonectt and Admin. No public signup.
- Coverage status must ultimately come from authoritative eligibility data, not inferred from visuals.
- Any unverified commercial data stays out of production copy.
